About the host

Hosted by Roorkee Institute of Technology

RIT Roorkee is a NAAC A++ graded institution — the first and only one in Uttarakhand to hold this rating — and ranks among the top 4% of institutions in India. CodeSprint is our open invitation to students across the college to spend a day building, not just studying.

Pick a track

Five categories, one afternoon

Open to BCA, BTech CSE, MCA and MTech students. Pick the track that fits your team's strengths — or take on Open Innovation with an idea of your own.

AI / ML

Predictive models, computer vision, NLP, recommendation systems — practical machine learning applications.

Cybersecurity

Vulnerability detection, secure-auth systems, threat monitoring tools — build for defense, not just demos.

Blockchain & Web3

Smart contracts, decentralized apps, wallets and on-chain tools for real, working use cases.

Web App Development

Full-stack products that solve a real problem — for the campus, a business, or the public.

Open Innovation

Any idea, any stack — if it solves a real problem, this is your track.
